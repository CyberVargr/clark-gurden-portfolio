06 · B2B & regional-sales enablement

Technical Product-Sheet Governance

Turning complex hardware configurations into governed source content for tenders, partners, regional sales, web, and retail.

Across 80+ regional technical product sheets annually, Clark did more than format information. He interpreted hardware specifications, reconciled variants and configuration limits, normalized terminology, governed claims and disclaimers, and adapted a structured source for different commercial and consumer audiences.

Across commercial systems, gaming hardware, and AI PCs, the work required the same discipline: separate universal product facts from model- or region-dependent details, preserve qualification language, and give each audience the fields needed to make a decision.
