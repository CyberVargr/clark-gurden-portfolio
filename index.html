Predator & Acer Nitro

The problem: Nitro needed to feel more accessible than Predator without reading as a compromised or “budget” version of it.

The decision: Applied distinct Predator and Acer Nitro positioning across handhelds, laptops, desktops, displays, and peripherals—keeping Nitro benefit-led, then using specifications as proof.

Clark's role: Owned global English messaging for the Nitro line and applied the brand logic through copymasters, KSP structures, product-page, retail, and regional source content.
